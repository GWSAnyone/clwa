# Market Square

Where merchants sell their wares. [[Mark]] and [[Old Bess]] have their stalls here.

## Regular Occupants
- [[Mark]] — morning trading
- [[Old Bess]] — morning/afternoon selling remedies and reading fortunes
- [[Elara]] — afternoon performing for shoppers

## Conversation Hook
Comment on the goods, the prices, or who you saw shopping today.

## Narrative Function
- **Primary crime scene location**: Mark and Bess are both here during the day
- Commerce hub: shop interactions happen here
- Bess reads fortunes here → potential player intel source
- Mark's fake spices are sold here → discoverable evidence
- Market days draw a few traders from surrounding farms
