# Millhaven

A small frontier village at a crossroads. Eight souls, each with secrets.

## Geography
- Founded over 200 years ago by settlers from the eastern kingdoms
- The road to Valdris (nearest city) is a week's journey through bandit-prone highlands
- Mostly self-sufficient. Market days draw a few traders from surrounding farms

## Locations
- [[Silver Flagon Tavern]] — social heart, 200+ years of ale
- [[Town Square]] — central hub, connected to everything
- [[Town Gate]] — only way in or out, [[Garrett]]'s post
- [[Market Square]] — commerce, [[Mark]] and [[Old Bess]] trade here
- [[Blacksmith]] — [[Hilda]]'s forge, built by her father
- [[Temple of Dawn]] — built by Brother Celwyn, [[Father Aldric]]'s charge
- [[Residential Lane]] — quiet homes
- [[Courthouse]] — [[Magistrate Theron]]'s domain, holding cell used maybe once a year

## Key Facts
- No formal jail — just a holding cell in the courthouse
- Winters are harsh, village depends on stored grain and Bess's herbs
- Everyone knows everyone. Secrets don't stay hidden long.
- Magistrate Theron was sent from the capital 5 years ago. Nobody knows why.
