# The World of Negation

> In-fiction primer. Канон мира (худтекст), не дизайн-док. Терминология и схема
> данных — в `CONCEPT_Negation.md` / `CONCEPT_Negation_Terminology.md`. Designation
> и народные ярлыки — контент-строки (INV-14).

## The People

The people of this world are **ordinary humans** — no other races, no beast-folk, no
inhuman bloodlines. The only anomaly the world holds is Negation, and the Relicts who
carry it. But these humans are not quite Earth's: they **feel law in the body.**

## The felt fabric of law

Here, law is not abstract. What is established — a custom, a right, the plain
regularity of things — registers as **sensation**: a warmth, a chill, an ache in some
particular place. Those who live by a law, even without thinking of it, **share the
feeling** when it is engaged or broken. Society holds not only by agreement but by a
**shared sense of rightness**, felt in the flesh. It runs as one fabric from the
shallow end (manners, mores) to the deep (the fundamental Rules of reality) — people
do not feel a seam between *the law that we mourn the dead* and *the law that the dead
stay gone.*

Reality, then, is held together by **Rules** — the fundamental laws at the deep end of
that fabric. They are not written; they simply hold, and are felt to hold. They hold
for everyone.

Except for the few they do not. Near a Relict, the shared feeling **falters** — which
is why people shy from them before any rumor: the body knows, before the mind, that a
law is tearing here.

## Negation

A **Negation** is the capacity to deny one Rule of reality. Not a gift, not a power —
a **consequence**. It is born from a break a person could not survive whole, and it
keeps the shape of that break forever. Near a Negation, one law of the world simply
**is not.** A wound that will not close. A voice that will not carry. A secret that
will not stay kept.

The world has noticed two forms, and does not believe they are the same thing:

- **Recluse** — the Negation turned **inward**. A wound so deep the mind refused a
  law, and reality bent **around the bearer.** They harm no one directly; reality is
  merely unstable near them. Pitied and feared, like lepers. *(Street: «тихие»,
  «стоячие».)*
- **Vector** — the Negation turned **outward**. The bearer wanted something with all
  they had; the world granted it and took a law in payment, and now they carry that
  debt **into everyone around them.** Not villains — debtors — but the world suffers
  near them. Feared like a contagion. *(Street: «течь», «порченые».)*

It is an **observed rule, not a proven law**, that no one has ever been both at once.

The bearers have their own word, spoken among themselves, that the system does not
recognize: **the Relict.** That which outlived its own time — the remnant of a
process that no longer runs. They feel a kinship the world denies them.

## What no one knows

The structure is known. The cause is not. No one knows **why** a Negation comes to
*these* people, whether it can be predicted, whether it can be caused or prevented,
or whether Recluse and Vector are truly the same thing wearing two faces. These are
the unanswered questions — and the only ones that matter.

## The Objects

There are facilities for the bearers — **the Objects** — and there are many, the
world over. They wear a respectable face: a **pharmaceutical company**, with branches
and real medicine, that claims to **treat** the bearers — to have found, or to be
finding, a cure. The true authority behind the company no resident ever sees;
directives arrive on paper, and the only faces of power are the staff. The Object
itself sits, sealed, inside a working clinic.

A bearer is brought in not when they become *dangerous*, but when they become
**known.** The moment word of a new Relict reaches the apparatus, they are taken — and
admitted, the public formula reads, **for treatment.** Visibility is the trigger. You
are not here because you harmed anyone. You are here because you were found.

There is no cure. There never has been — no one knows how. Each Object is, beneath the
hospital face, three things at once:
- a **refuge** — outside, a frightened world would rather they were gone;
- a **prison** — there is no leaving;
- a **workplace** — quietly, the Object **uses** what it contains, and offers the
  suitable **contracts** — work in the world (often hunting their own kind) for better
  conditions, never freedom.

## Outside

Beyond the sealed wing is the ordinary world — one that **knows** the bearers exist
and fears them, and is grateful for a clean answer: not a mob, a *clinic*. They are
being treated, the world tells itself, and looks away. Where that story frays, the
older ugliness shows — denouncement, the crowd, the wish to be rid of them. To most
residents, outside is the thing that found them and the lie that took them; the Object
is the lesser cruelty. What the apparatus truly wants with the bearers it gathers, no
one inside has been told.
