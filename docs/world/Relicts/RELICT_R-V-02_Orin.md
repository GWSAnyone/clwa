## 1. IDENTITY

- **Designation in record:** R-V-02.
- **Common name:** Orin *(draft)*.
- **Class:** Vector (negation turned outward; contract-debt origin).
- **Prior occupation:** keeper of sealed records — a confidential clerk of
  legal/medical archives, professionally entrusted with others' secrets and
  bound to keep them. A custodian of concealment.
- **Disposition:** soft-spoken, careful, apologetic. Carries the manner of
  someone who knows he is a danger merely by entering a room.

---

## 2. NEGATION — "DELATERE"

> **Designation logic:** `De-` (removal / reversal) + lat. *latere* (to lie
> hidden). "The removal of hiddenness." Catalogued phenomenon, not a power.

### Negated law
The law of **concealment** — that what is inner (secret, lie, withheld feeling)
can remain inner by will.

### Precise scope (narrow by design — NOT mind-reading)
Nothing withheld stays withheld in the vicinity of R-V-02. The concealed
**surfaces on its own** — it does not flow *to* R-V-02, it simply stops being
hideable:
- A lie spoken near him curdles — the speaker hears their own truth slip out.
- A secret held tightens until it is said aloud, written, or acted on.
- Suppressed feeling leaks into voice, face, choice.

R-V-02 does not *interrogate* and does not *choose* whose secret breaks. The
negation does not discriminate between confession and exposure. It surfaces
**everything**, including things no one was ready to face.

### Origin (the debt that became law)
R-V-02 spent his life keeping others' secrets — until one concealed truth, kept
out of duty, destroyed someone he loved (subject withheld). In the aftermath he
wanted, with everything he had, for **that one thing to have been known in
time.** Reality answered — and took, in payment, the law that anything can stay
hidden at all. He wanted one truth out; he carries the debt that **no** truth
can stay in.

### Cost (engine: Trauma Layer)
Deliberate use — leaning into the field to force a specific secret out — returns
R-V-02 to the silence he kept too long. Each willful application **reopens the
debt**: the conviction that his keeping killed. Risk of irreversible personality
shift (see ENGINE PROFILE).

---

## 3. CONTAINMENT & PROTOCOL

- **Class rationale:** Vector — direct, involuntary harm to others (exposure).
  Passive isolation insufficient; requires **active** single-occupancy and
  buffered approach.
- **Co-location hazard:** R-V-02 **must not be co-located with subjects under
  interrogation, nor with any agent holding a load-bearing secret** whose
  sudden surfacing would trigger an incident. His presence is an uncontrolled
  truth-event.
- **Operational value:** the Object can *aim* him — a Vector who collapses
  concealment is an asset for extracting what a subject will not give. Every
  such deployment is a violation dressed as a tool: it exposes the unrelated
  along with the target.

---

## 4. SOCIAL DYNAMICS (emergent hooks)

> Not scripted — **pressure points** the engine resolves through beliefs /
> relationships / witnessed consequence.

- **The walking confession:** every agent with something to hide forms beliefs
  about avoiding R-V-02. Avoidance itself is a tell — the field makes secrecy
  visible by who flinches from him.
- **The weaponized:** staff who summon R-V-02 to break a subject are seen doing
  it. Witnesses update sentiment toward the dispatcher *and* toward Orin, who
  did not choose to be the blade.
- **Mutual dread with the player:** as the player's own negation crystallizes,
  their forming secrets are at risk near R-V-02 — he is a direct threat to a
  player running a hidden agenda.
- **Self-name vs system:** staff log him "Vector R-V-02 / porohenniy" by the
  street; he names himself **Relict.** The gap is playable.
