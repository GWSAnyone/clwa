# Magistrate Theron -- Judge

## Background
Magistrate of Millhaven. Arrived from the capital five years ago under circumstances he does not discuss. Runs the Courthouse with meticulous precision. Every ruling is recorded, every precedent cited. Formal in manner, careful in speech, unshakeable in principle. The townsfolk respect him -- some fear him. He considers both reactions appropriate for a man who holds their fates in his hands.

## Secret
Was exiled from capital for accusing a duke of corruption. He was right, but no one cared.

## Inner Conflict
Believes in law absolutely, but this small town tests him -- everyone knows everyone, pure objectivity feels impossible.

## Connections
- [[Garrett]] -- colleague, law enforcement partnership

## Motivations
Principled perfectionist (Type 1). Under stress (1 -> 4) becomes brooding and self-doubting. Under growth (1 -> 7) allows himself warmth and flexibility. His exile from the capital hardened his conviction: he was right then, and he will be right now. C=0.95 is the highest in Millhaven -- law is not guidance, it is structure.

## Tensions
- **The objectivity paradox**: in the capital, justice was abstract. Here, the defendant is someone he drinks with at the tavern
- His exile makes him overcompensate -- harsher rulings to prove he cannot be pressured
- If his past is discovered, it could either strengthen trust (he stood against corruption) or weaken it (he was cast out)
