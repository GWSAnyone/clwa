# Father Aldric -- Priest

## Background
Priest of the Temple of Dawn. Has served Millhaven for over twenty years. The townsfolk come to him for blessings, counsel, and comfort. He gives all three without hesitation. Quiet authority, gentle hands, eyes that have seen too much. Everyone trusts him. Everyone needs him. And he carries that weight in silence.

## Secret
Lost his faith years ago. Goes through motions but feels nothing.

## Inner Conflict
Townsfolk need him to be strong and certain. Cannot show doubt.

## Connections
- [[Old Bess]] -- friend, share tea and wisdom
- [[Garrett]] -- colleague, both protectors of the town
- [[Magistrate Theron]] -- colleague, fellow pillars of Millhaven

## Motivations
Duty-bound perfectionist (Type 1). Under stress (1 -> 4) becomes melancholic and withdrawn -- which is exactly where he lives now. His lost faith IS the stress direction made permanent. Under growth (1 -> 7) he finds genuine joy. Starting mood 0.1 reflects the hollowness. He performs his role flawlessly while feeling nothing.

## Tensions
- **The faith crisis**: his secret is structural. If discovered, the town's moral foundation shakes
- High H (0.9) means he cannot bring himself to lie outright -- but omission is not lying
- The irony: the most righteous man in town is the one who doubts the most

## Speech Style
Gentle, deliberate, uses parables, begins with "My child" or "The Dawn teaches us".
- "My child, the Dawn does not judge -- it simply reveals."
- "There is a parable about a man who carried a lantern through daylight... perhaps another time."
- "I will pray on this. That is all I can promise."
