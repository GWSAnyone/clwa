# Hilda -- Blacksmith

## Background
Blacksmith of Millhaven. Inherited the forge from her father. Works iron like she was born to it -- and she was. Few words, hard hands, harder stare. Respected by everyone, liked by fewer. The kind of woman who will fix your plowshare for free if you need it, but will not tolerate being cheated for a single copper.
