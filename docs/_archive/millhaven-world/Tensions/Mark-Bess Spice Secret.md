# Mark-Bess Spice Secret

**The core tension of Millhaven.** Most likely to escalate into the session's main crime.

## Initial Conditions
- [[Mark]] sells fake spices (common herbs marked up tenfold)
- [[Old Bess]] knows. Has known since week one. Keeps hinting.
- Mark → Bess: sentiment **-0.7**, trust **0.0**
- Mark: H=0.2 (dishonest), mood -0.2 (uneasy), N=0.6 (anxious)
- Mark has `motive: { targetId: "bess" }`

## Why It Escalates
Mark is trapped: Bess knows his secret, his debts to Valdris are growing, and he can't leave because he has nowhere else to go. Every day Bess smiles that knowing smile, the pressure builds. His low H means he'll consider criminal solutions. His Enneagram 3w2 under stress → Type 9 (indifference) means he might act without remorse.

## Possible Outcomes
- **Murder**: Mark kills Bess to silence her (most common with low H + high negative mood)
- **Blackmail**: Mark threatens Bess to keep quiet (if N is moderate)
- **Theft**: Mark steals from Bess's stall (lesser escalation)
- **Prevention**: Player warns Bess, or confronts Mark, or alerts Garrett
- **Natural resolution**: If Mark's mood improves (player helps him), tension defuses

## Evidence If Crime Occurs
- Opportunity: Mark and Bess co-located at Market Square mornings
- Motive: Mark's sentiment -0.7 toward Bess (discoverable through observation)
- Physical: traces of merchant goods near crime scene
- Bess's knowledge of fake spices → motive confirmed by asking any NPC who knew

## Player Discovery Path
1. Talk to Bess → she hints about "someone's goods not being what they seem"
2. Talk to Mark → defensive about his business
3. Ask Ted → mentions Mark's debts
4. Observe Mark's behavior → avoids Bess, nervous when she's mentioned
5. Examine spices (if economy module active) → confirm they're fake
