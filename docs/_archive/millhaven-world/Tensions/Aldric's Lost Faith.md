# Aldric's Lost Faith

## Initial Conditions
- [[Father Aldric]] lost his faith 5 years ago. Feels nothing when he prays.
- Goes through the motions — candles, hymns, sermons. Inside: silence.
- [[Old Bess]] senses something is wrong but never asks
- They share evening tea — the only honest relationship he has
- Mood: 0.1 (barely positive — fragile)

## Why It Matters
- Aldric is the moral center of the village. If he breaks → ripple effects
- Player confiding in Aldric about the crime → he may break down instead of helping
- If crime happens near the temple → Aldric's crisis deepens
- Under stress (Enneagram 1→4): becomes mournful, withdrawn — less helpful

## Possible Outcomes
- **Dormant**: Aldric holds it together, helps in his limited way
- **Crisis**: a traumatic event (murder of Bess, his friend) shatters his facade
- **Growth**: player or event pushes him toward Enneagram growth (1→7: joy, acceptance)
- **Confession**: Aldric tells player his secret → deep character moment, but doesn't help investigation
