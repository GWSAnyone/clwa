# Bess's Vision of the Murder

## Initial Conditions
- [[Old Bess]] can genuinely see glimpses of the future in tea leaves
- She **saw the murder before it happened** and said nothing
- Inner conflict: could she have warned them? Would anyone have believed her?
- The guilt visits her every night

## Why It Matters
- **Anonymous tip candidate**: Bess is the most likely sender of the anonymous note
- If player discovers her gift → she becomes the most valuable witness
- But she withheld information → morally ambiguous (complicit by silence?)
- Her knowledge is cryptic, not direct — "the leaves showed shadows, not names"

## Possible Outcomes
- **Tipster revealed**: player discovers Bess sent the note → deeper context about the crime
- **Confrontation**: player accuses Bess of complicity → she breaks down
- **Ally**: player gains her trust → she shares visions (unreliable, fragmented, but useful)
- **Victim**: if Bess is the murder victim → her vision was about herself. Tragic irony.

## Design Note
Bess's vision mechanic is powerful but must stay ambiguous. She doesn't see names or exact events — fragments, feelings, colors. This prevents her from being a "walkthrough NPC" who tells you everything.
