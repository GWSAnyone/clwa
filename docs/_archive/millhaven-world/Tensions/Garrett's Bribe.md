# Garrett's Past Bribe

## Initial Conditions
- [[Garrett]] let a fugitive pass through the gate for a bribe last winter
- No one knows — it's his deepest secret
- Inner conflict: prides himself on duty, but that one lapse haunts him
- Overreacts when integrity is questioned

## Why It Matters
- If player discovers this → leverage over Garrett (blackmail potential)
- If Garrett is randomized as corrupt (low H) → this past event becomes a pattern, not an anomaly
- If Garrett is principled (high H) → the guilt makes him MORE determined, but also vulnerable
- Elara knows about the bribe (her memories mention it) → she could expose him

## Possible Outcomes
- **Hidden**: stays buried, affects Garrett's behavior subtly
- **Player discovers**: through Elara, or by pressing Garrett during investigation
- **Exposed**: town loses trust in guard → chaos, especially during active crime
- **Used**: player blackmails Garrett → gains cooperation but destroys trust system
