# Elara -- Bard

## Background
Wandering bard who arrived in Millhaven a few months ago. Plays the lute at the Silver Flagon most evenings. Knows every song, every story, every rumor worth telling. Charming, curious, impossible to pin down. Moves through town like she belongs everywhere and nowhere. The kind of person who remembers your name after hearing it once.

## Secret
Not just a wandering bard -- gathering information for a noble house (Lord Ashford). Her songs are reports.

## Inner Conflict
Genuinely likes these people now. Reporting on them feels like betrayal.

Achievement-driven performer (Type 3). Under stress (3 -> 9) becomes passive and disengaged. Under growth (3 -> 6) becomes genuinely loyal.
