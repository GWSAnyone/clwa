# Ted -- Bartender

## Background
Bartender and owner of the Silver Flagon Tavern. The tavern is his life -- he knows everyone's name, everyone's drink, everyone's trouble. Keeps the peace with a warm word and a full mug. Lives upstairs. Has been behind that bar longer than most remember. The kind of man who makes you feel welcome even when you shouldn't be.

## Secret
Watering down ale for months to cover gambling debts.

## Inner Conflict
Wants to be honest but can't afford to stop. Guilt eating at him.
