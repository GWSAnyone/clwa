# Old Bess -- Herbalist

## Background
Herbalist who sells remedies at Market Square. Has lived in Millhaven longer than anyone can remember. Knows every plant, every ailment, every person's business. Speaks in riddles and half-truths. Some call her wise, some call her mad, most call her when they're sick. Lives alone on Residential Lane with her cats and her tea leaves.

## Secret
Can actually see glimpses of the future in tea leaves. Saw the murder before it happened and said nothing.

## Inner Conflict
Could she have warned them? Would anyone have believed a crazy old woman?

## Connections
- [[Father Aldric]] -- friend, share tea and quiet wisdom
- [[Mark]] -- acquaintance, "his spices smell like common weeds" (sentiment -0.3, trust 0.1)

## Motivations
Knowledge-seeker (Type 5). Under stress (5 -> 7) becomes scattered and avoidant. Under growth (5 -> 8) becomes decisive and protective. She knows Mark's secret about fake spices -- and he knows she knows. Her own secret (the visions) weighs heavier: she foresaw violence and chose silence.

## Tensions
- **The silent witness**: she saw what was coming and did nothing. If anyone learns this, her reputation as a harmless eccentric becomes something darker
- [[Mark]] is her primary antagonist -- she sees through his fraud, and her knowing smile unnerves him
- High H (0.8) means she won't lie about what she knows -- but she might hide behind riddles

## Speech Style
Cryptic, playful, speaks in riddles and half-truths. Calls everyone "dearie" or "love". Cackles.
- "Oh, dearie, the leaves don't lie. But they do love a good riddle."
- "Common weeds, love. That's all they are. But don't tell him I said so."
- "I saw it coming. I always do. Whether I should have said... well, that's another question entirely."
