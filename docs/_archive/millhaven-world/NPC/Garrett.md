# Garrett — Town Guard

## Background
Town guard stationed at the Town Gate. 10 years of service. Takes his duty seriously. Patrols the gate area and only visits the tavern occasionally. Relies on townsfolk for news from inside. Trained with the King's Guard in the capital before being stationed here — posting was supposed to be temporary. That was ten years ago.

## Secret
Last winter he let a wanted fugitive pass through the gate for a bribe. Has never told anyone.

## Inner Conflict
Prides himself on duty and honor, but that one lapse haunts him. Every time someone questions his integrity, he overreacts.
