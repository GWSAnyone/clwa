Royal Court Romance & Intrigue (deep-social, non-violent): courtship, romantic bonds, alliances, scheming for monarch's favor, flattering/damaging rumors; win = social standing / marriage.

- Custom multi-step social actions (propose_marriage, form_alliance, swear_fealty, denounce) via registerStepHandler + registerActionVerbs; engine resolveTemplates handles arbitrary module step actions generically.
- Rumor-as-influence: IL Claim->belief in applyDialogueMeaning is content-agnostic — gated only by isTransferable(eventType) registry flag + trust scaling -> TESTIMONY belief. NPC->NPC transferBeliefs + registerDayAdvanceHandler (the crime module's rumor spreader) are the exact hooks. registerBeliefCreator('RUMOR_SPREAD',...) already exists.
- Social ripples / consequence cascades: ConsequenceRule conditions already support op:'relationship' with selectable field (sentiment|trust), comparator, actor/toward, plus trait/belief/has_capability and targetMode witnesses|location|affected|all. ## verdict

occasional-engine: ~80% of the genre (events, affordances, rumor-as-belief-influence, CG social ripples via op:'relationship', multi-party goals, ambition/love motivation) is genuinely module-only — the registries and the generic belief/CG/volition pipelines hold up. But the genre's WIN CONDITION (social standing / favor) has no engine-native scalar, relationship bond-types are a closed union (no 'alliance'), and the engine's prompt prose (role-directive + belief summaries) is crime-framed — so you would touch the engine a handful of times, mostly to add a universal standing axis + bond-type registry and to de-hardcode two prose builders.

## confidence

high — every claim traced to specific files/lines (relationships.ts RelationType union + getGriefPenalty switch; agent.ts buildRoleDirective L376-405 crime prose; beliefs.ts 'committed X against Y'; il.ts EmotionalPressure union vs getRegisteredPressureTypes schema; cg-engine op:'relationship' field-selectable; volition/al utility dimensions; crime+social module index registration surface).

## notes

Distinguish two failure modes clearly: (1) GENUINE universal gaps the engine SHOULD grow regardless of genre — a relationship graph that stores only sentiment+trust with a closed bond-type union and no standing/rank axis is under-built for ANY social MMO (politics, guilds, factions all need it), so adding registry-backed bond types + a standing scalar is a legit new-universal-capability, not a crime concession. (2) TRUE crime leaks — buildRoleDirective and belief 'committed/against' phrasing are crime-justice interpretations sitting in the engine; they violate INV-14's spirit (zero module-specific content) and would mis-render for this genre. The architecture's core bet (string event types + 40+ registries + generic CG relationship/trait/belief conditions + PM-utility-then-LLM volition) genuinely pays off here: rumor mechanics, scheming, alliances, and courtship affordances all register without engine edits. The non-violent focus is NOT a problem — nothing in volition/al/cg requires violence; severity/lethal are optional fields. The real friction is social-state richness (standing/favor/bond-type) and prose neutrality, not behavioral physics. Recommend: (a) replace RelationType union with a registry + add optional `standing`/`favor` numeric to Relationship (universal), (b) add registerRoleDirectiveBuilder + registerBeliefPhrasing hooks to drain the two prose leaks, (c) make EmotionalPressure.type = string. After those, this genre is module-only.
