# Elara's Espionage

## Initial Conditions
- [[Elara]] is a spy for Lord Ashford, posing as a wandering bard
- [[Garrett]] is suspicious: "something about her does not add up" (sentiment -0.1, trust 0.3)
- Elara is wary of Garrett (sentiment -0.1, trust 0.3)
- Elara: H=0.5 (borderline), inner conflict about betraying people she's grown to like

## Why It Matters
- Garrett investigating Elara creates parallel investigation to the main crime
- If player befriends Elara → she might confide her secret → trust dilemma
- If Elara's cover is blown → she flees (unique event) or turns on her patron
- Elara knows everyone's secrets from performing at the tavern → valuable witness OR unreliable one

## Possible Outcomes
- **Undiscovered**: Elara continues spying, background tension only
- **Player discovers**: confrontation, blackmail opportunity, or helping her escape
- **Garrett discovers**: arrest or confrontation — complicates main investigation
- **Elara confesses**: under emotional pressure, reveals truth → town reacts
