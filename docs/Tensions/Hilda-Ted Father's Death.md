# Hilda-Ted: Father's Death

## Initial Conditions
- [[Hilda]]'s father died in a bar fight at [[Ted]]'s tavern
- Ted broke it up "too late" — Hilda never blamed him openly
- Hilda → Ted: type "friend", but sentiment **-0.2**, trust 0.4
- Hilda has `motive: { targetId: "ted" }`
- They drink together every evening — friendship layered over resentment

## Why It Matters
- Hilda is a secondary crime candidate (especially with randomized low A or high N)
- Even without crime: this tension creates rich dialogue moments
- Player asking Hilda about Ted → she deflects but body language reveals something
- If Ted is hurt or killed by someone else → Hilda's reaction is complex (grief + secret relief?)

## Possible Outcomes
- **Dormant** (most common): friendship holds, resentment stays buried
- **Confrontation**: under stress, Hilda confronts Ted → emotional scene
- **Violence**: very unlikely with current traits, but possible with randomized low H + high N
- **Resolution**: if player mediates or triggers honest conversation
